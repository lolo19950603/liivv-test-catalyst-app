## How to mark each kit

Under each kit there is a decision line. Tick one box and write any changes on the line below it:

- **Approve**: list the kit as it is.
- **Change**: list it after the changes you write (add, remove or swap an item, rename, reword).
- **Withhold**: keep it off the site. It stays hidden until you approve it.

Nothing is switched on or off until you return this. The "Earlier plan" column is the suggestion from the 2026-10-05 plan, for reference only; your mark decides.

## What you should know first

- **All 12 kits are visible in the store today** (shop shelf and search), even though the Diabetes Care landing shows none until you sign them off. "Withhold" means hiding the kit product in BigCommerce.
- **Kits do not track stock.** A kit can be bought even when an item inside it is out of stock. Six items are out of stock today; they are marked **OUT OF STOCK** below.
- **Quantities.** Every item goes in at quantity 1 (one box, bottle or pack). The customer can change quantities or remove items before checkout.
- **Options.** No kit locks a size or colour, so each item uses its first option (shown in the Option column).
- **Kit price** was set when the kits were made, as the sum of the item prices then. The table shows today's sum beside it.
- **Brand.** Most items have no brand set in the store. Where it is blank there, the maker is taken from the product name and marked with \*.
- **Listing links** are to liivv.ca.
- **Sources** [S1]–[S16] are listed at the end. I opened each one on 2026-10-06 and quoted its exact words. Anything not from an official source is labelled as a question for you.
