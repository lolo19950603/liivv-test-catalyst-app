11 · FAQ — diabetes-care-faq-first

Good questions, honest answers

The things people actually ask us — answered like a friend would, not a form letter.

Is this only for one type of diabetes?

Not at all. Liivv Diabetes covers Essentials, Every Day Living, New to the Journey, and Your Diabetes Journey — with paths for Type 1, Type 2, Gestational, and Prediabetes. You pick what fits; we follow your lead.

What's the difference between Essentials and Every Day Living?

Essentials is supplies and restock — meters, strips, sensors, and the staples you shouldn't scramble for. Every Day Living is food, movement, and lifestyle rhythm for the hours between appointments.

I'm newly diagnosed — where should I start?

Start with New to the Journey for calm basics, then browse Essentials when you're ready to stock up. Your Diabetes Journey can help if you already know your path.

How private is my order?

Very. Everything arrives in plain, discreet packaging, and your conversations with our team stay between you and us. What you order is nobody's business but yours.

What can I actually chat with a pharmacist about?

Everyday stuff — supply questions, "is this normal?" moments, and practical concerns. In Ontario, our pharmacists can assess and help with 19+ everyday concerns in chat during business hours (until 5 p.m. Eastern). Olivia is available anytime for orders, products, and account help — she does not give medical advice.

What's a CarePack?

Your daily essentials, organized by day and dose into one tidy pack — so mornings start with one small rip instead of a shelf of bottles. Set it once and it keeps arriving.

Can I change or pause my routine anytime?

Always. Life shifts, and your Liivv should shift with it. Swap products, skip a month, or pause entirely — no phone calls, no guilt trips.
