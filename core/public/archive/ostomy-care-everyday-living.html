11 · FAQ — diabetes-care-faq-first

Good questions, honest answers

The things people actually ask us — answered like a friend would, not a form letter.

Do you carry my preferred brand?

We stock popular options including Coloplast, Hollister, and Convatec — and we're happy to help if you're not sure yet. Browse Ostomy Essentials or ask Olivia for product help anytime.

I'm new — where should I start?

Start with New to the Journey for calm basics, then explore Essentials when you're ready to stock. Get to Know Your Stoma helps with understanding; Every Day Living covers life beyond supplies.

What's the difference between Essentials and Every Day Living?

Essentials is pouches, barriers, skin care, and restock. Every Day Living is clothing, travel, workdays, and confidence for the hours between changes.

How private is my order?

Very. Everything arrives in plain, discreet packaging, and your conversations with our team stay between you and us. What you order is nobody's business but yours.

What can I actually chat with a pharmacist about?

Everyday stuff — fit questions, skin comfort, "is this normal?" moments. In Ontario, our pharmacists can assess and help with 19+ everyday concerns in chat during business hours (until 5 p.m. Eastern). Olivia is available anytime for orders, products, and account help — she does not give medical advice.

What's a CarePack?

Your daily essentials, organized into one tidy pack — so mornings start simpler. Set it once and it keeps arriving.

Can I change or pause my routine anytime?

Always. Life shifts, and your Liivv should shift with it. Swap products, skip a month, or pause entirely — no phone calls, no guilt trips.
